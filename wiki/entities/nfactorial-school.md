## Operating model (from fireside chat)

1. **Selection**: ~4,000 applications → top ~100 to Almaty (numbers as stated by speaker; unverified).
2. **Intensity**: multi-week full-time build (8–12 weeks, 50–60 h/week claimed).
3. **Output contract**: ship product + get real users + **monetize** (even $1–2 proves willingness to pay).
4. **Feedback surfaces**: leaderboards for revenue, users, social reach.
5. **Curriculum**: rewritten frequently (weekly) to match new models/workflows.
6. **Asset**: multi-year alumni community; founder interest in what cohort does at age 40 (company-creation age stats).

## Why revenue is the north-star metric (their framing)

- Money = stored potential energy of the buyer.
- Paying = causal evidence the product reduces real pain.
- Family/friends compliments fail this test.

## Causal relevance for `pro/plan`

- Strong template for **efficiency metric**: impact proven by payment / retention, not pitch quality.
- Selection + intensity + public leaderboards = high signal environment (similar spirit to competitive programming objectivity).
- Internal tooling ambition (second brain, proactive advisor) mirrors vault/agent goals.
