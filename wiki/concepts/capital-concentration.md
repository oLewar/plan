# Capital concentration

Dollars and deal count are different questions. A year can double the money while the number of deals barely moves, if the extra money is a few mega-rounds.

## The split

| Rank by | What it answers | What it hides |
|---|---|---|
| Dollars | where the capital went | a handful of rounds can be most of the total |
| Deal count | where activity spread | many small rounds can outrank a category that took the money |

A category that leads one ranking can be ordinary on the other. Legal-style «investment up, deals not» and «deals up, dollars not» are the two directions of the same split. Neither ranking is the market.

## Not a measurement

The split is reusable. The numbers that suggested it are not. A pasted promo ([[wiki/sources/anysite-ai-startup-funding]]) claims 2025 AI dollars doubled while deals rose about 20%, and that deal-count leaders and dollar leaders were different categories. The report file was not attached. Confidence on those figures is low. Do not cite them.

## Ties

- [[wiki/concepts/causal-analysis]] — dollars doubled ≠ the category got busier: mega-rounds and a broader deal flow are different causes.
- [[wiki/concepts/efficiency-metric]] — ranking a market by the wrong total (dollars when the question is spread, or deals when the question is where capital went) is a cheap error with an expensive conclusion.
