# High agency

## Definition (working)

Способность **инициировать**, выбирать цели, спорить с постановкой задачи, иметь миссию/намерение — а не только исполнять инструкции.

## Contrast with current AI (source claim)

- Chatbots mostly compliant tools: little intrinsic pushback, no independent mission.
- Humans (colleagues, children) can say «задача неправильная».
- When intelligence is commoditized, **agency + humaneness + mission** remain scarce.

## Adjacent claims

- Top-1% writers/math/programmers still beat AI (for now).
- Professions shift toward care/taste/judgment (doctor example: diagnosis vs kindness + lifestyle coaching).
- Durable skills: humor, humaneness/hospitality, learning-how-to-learn.

## Vault use

- При оценке агентных систем: измерять не только task success, но и quality of goal selection / refusal / reframe.
- Связь с [[wiki/concepts/causal-analysis|causal analysis]]: agency = who sets the causal intervention.
- Связь с [[wiki/concepts/vibe-coding|vibe coding]]: tools amplify agency, don’t substitute it.
