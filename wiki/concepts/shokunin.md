# Shokunin (職人気質)

## Definition (working)

Японский идеал **мастера-ремесленника**, посвятившего жизнь совершенствованию одного дела (source anecdote: potter in Nara — unfinished craft continues in next life).

## Why it surfaces in AI talk

- Counterweight to vibe-coding democratization.
- Claim: tools spread; **taste + lifelong craft** stay scarce and employable across chemistry/physics/biology/software.
- Aligns with «no ego, keep learning» and multi-generation mastery.

## Vault relevance

- Quality bar for agents/products: not just ship speed, but craft integrity.
- Complements [[wiki/concepts/high-agency|high agency]] (direction) with **depth** (execution excellence).
- Pairs with [[wiki/concepts/barbell-strategy|barbell]]: timeless craft + new instruments.
